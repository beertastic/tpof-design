# Scene Elements

**Extracted 2026-08-01 from the Filmanize breakdown, immediately before that PDF
was deleted.** The screenplay lists no props, set dressings or costumes anywhere —
Filmanize derived them, and this was the only copy.

It earns its place: the `Costumes: Jedi Robes` entry on Scene 12 is how the art
department discovered the script names a costume there at all, and the
`Visual Effects: Krellis head shot` flag is the only pre-existing note that the
hardest shot in the film needs planning.

## How much to trust it

**The descriptions and element lists are reliable.** Verified against the
screenplay before deletion: the per-scene page numbers line up scene for scene,
the whole way through.

**The numbering of the last two scenes is not, and is left as the export had it.**
The export splits the finale into `24` and `24A`. **The screenplay has no 24A** —
it has Sc.24, the clearing fight where Vala dies, and Sc.25, the finale. The two
splits do not correspond: the export puts the entire ensemble in both of its final
scenes. Everything up to Scene 23 maps one-to-one.

**Scene 6** is listed here as an ordinary forest scene. In the screenplay it is a
vision.

**Lengths are in eighths of a page**, and are the export's measurement.

**Where this disagrees with the screenplay, the screenplay wins** — see
[`scenes/`](scenes/).

## A note on the text

The export's PDF put a spurious space after every lowercase `w`, so the
descriptions read *"bed dow n w ith patrols"*. That has been repaired here. If a
word looks joined, it is a repair artefact, not the original.
