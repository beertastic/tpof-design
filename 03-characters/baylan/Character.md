# Baylan

> **Production rule:** Refer to the Production Design Bible before any visual development or image generation.

> **Naming.** `Baylan` and `Bay` are the same character — confirmed by the
> Production Designer, 2026-07-30. `Baylan` is the canonical asset name; `Bay`
> is the short form used through most of the script. The Filmanize breakdown
> credits both names separately in Scene 24A, which inflates that scene's
> character count from 12 to 13. Do not create a second character folder.

## Art Department Brief

Point-of-view character carried through the mercenary camp, the night forest and the morning pursuit. Highest scene count in the film.

## Story Function

TBD. Scene 3 opens on Baylan alone in the forest. Scene 5 finds him with eyes closed in an evening forest, "focusing on a new sound after years of reliving his past". Scene 7 has him waking from a vision as Nyx presses him into unloading the ship and making camp — Nyx taunts that escapees won't get far at night, and Baylan watches, shaken, from the clearing's edge. He appears in the Sand Dunes Force-vision (Scene 12) alongside Palpatine and Shin.

## Open Questions

- Is Baylan a mercenary, a captive, or moving between the two? The breakdown places him inside the mercenary camp but frames him as an observer, and Nyx orders him to work. His faction assignment drives the entire costume language.
- What is the past he has spent years reliving (Scene 5), and does it leave a visible mark — scarring, a kept object, a piece of costume that does not belong with the rest?
- The Force-vision in Scene 12 places him with Palpatine and Shin. Does his design need to work in that non-naturalistic register as well as in the forest?
