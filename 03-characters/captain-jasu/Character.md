# Captain Jasu

> **Production rule:** Refer to the Production Design Bible before any visual development or image generation.

## Art Department Brief

Mercenary captain and field commander.

## Story Function

Commands the mercenary crew hunting the escapees. Briefs the camp (Sc.9), briefs
the squad at dawn (Sc.19), hands Baylan the scanner (Sc.20), and splits the crew
for the pursuit (Sc.23). In the finale she **sets her akk dog on Jeyin** and calls
it off afterwards.

### The two things she does in the finale, and they define her

**She kills Krellis.** Not from cover, not at distance. She walks up beside a man
making a speech about mercy and puts a bolt through his head while he is still
talking — then **smiles**. Everything else about her should be built so that reads
as characteristic rather than shocking.

**She sets the animal she loves on a dying woman.** Sc.9 establishes that the
affection is genuine; the finale is her spending something she loves to make a
point to a fifteen-year-old. She calls it off afterwards, and it comes.

### How she dies — **corrected 2026-08-01**

**Not by Baylan.** The design documents said he kills everyone; he does not kill
her.

> A blaster fires from behind them and, without looking, **Bay deflects it from
> his lightsaber. It hits Jasu in the chest**, and she falls to the ground,
> unmoving.

**Nyx kills his own captain by accident, through the man he is shooting at**, and
nobody in the film ever remarks on it. Nyx does not appear to realise, and he is
dead a page later.

She charges Baylan first — *"filled with confusion and rage"* — after the akk dog
refuses her and goes to him. **The last thing that happens to her before she dies
is her own animal choosing somebody else.**

### She commands because of the akk dog

**Her authority is borrowed from an animal.** Not rank, not seniority, not
tactical skill, not the crew's respect — she is in charge because she owns a
monster and everyone knows it.

This is the central fact of her design.

- **She must not look like a natural leader.** No insignia that commands respect,
  no meaningful rank marking, no bearing of authority. Whatever she projects is
  on loan from the beast at her side.
- **The akk dog is the rank.** Where she is, it is.
- **It adores her, and she almost certainly raised it from young** — implied, never
  stated. That complicates Scene 9 considerably: the petting is *genuine*. She is
  not performing affection for an audience of subordinates, she is fond of her
  animal, and the reminder to everyone watching is a by-product.
- Which makes the finale worse rather than better. **She spends something she
  loves.** Setting it on Jeyin and calling it back is not the cold use of a
  weapon — it is a person using something that trusts her, and then keeping it.
- Scene 19's kneeling squad is compliance through fear, not discipline.
- She probably takes the largest share, so she may be the **best-equipped** of
  them. Money without authority is a specific and useful look.
- In the finale she uses her instrument of power on an unarmed, injured woman —
  and then Baylan removes the whole structure in under two minutes.

### What the script fixes, and the documents were not using

> **CAPTAIN JASU (40s), a powerful woman with a mean streak.** — Sc.9, identical
> in v9 and v10.

So: **human woman, forties, powerfully built.** Not an art department decision.

**She has taken one of Nyx's eyes.** Sc.14, Nyx to Baylan, lightly, as a joke
between friends: *"Gotta finish my route or Jasu will have my other eye."* That
it is said as a joke is the point — the crew has normalised being maimed by her.
**NYX'S DESIGN MUST CARRY AN EYE INJURY SHE CAUSED.**

**She and Baylan are old friends.** Tatooine, missions past, they laugh together
in Sc.9 and sit comfortably in the memory. The finale is not a man killing his
commander; it is a man destroying someone he likes, inside the only safety he
has ever had.

**Scene 9 already plants the finale.** The akk dog gets up and lies down beside
Bay instead of her, and Jasu watches it happen. In Sc.25 *"her own animal
choosing somebody else"* is the last thing that happens to her before she dies.
**It is the same beat, twice** — recorded in `02-story/Planted-Elements.md`.

**The akk dog is FEMALE in the script** — *"She hasn't eaten," "She needs to
hunt."* Correct any document that says "it".

## Costume note

No uniform exists in this crew — see
[`04-factions/mercenaries/Faction.md`](../../04-factions/mercenaries/Faction.md).
Everyone assembled their own kit; they are as mismatched as the escapees, just
better equipped and by choice.

Hers should read as **expensive rather than commanding.** Good materials,
maintained hardware, the best boots in the crew — and nothing that a real officer
would wear.

## Design Intent — **decided 2026-08-01**

**She has bought the shape of authority because she has none of the real thing.**

The brief for her costume was *expensive rather than commanding*, and the
resolution is that she dresses as an officer of an army she was never in. In a
crew where nothing matches and everything is scavenged, **she is the only person
in the film wearing a coherent, deliberately assembled outfit** — and that is
exactly what makes her look like she is playing at it. Nothing was issued to her.
Nothing was earned. The ornate band at her brow is *chosen*.

It should be very slightly absurd, and nobody is going to say so, because of the
animal. This is how "she must not look like a natural leader" and "she is the
best-equipped of them" stop contradicting each other.

### Where the shape came from, and the rule it had to satisfy

The reference is a Japanese army officer, battle-hardened but elegant. Bible
§6.5 forbids designs that "appear to originate from a recognisable period of
Earth history", so **only the proportions transfer** — high closed collar, hard
square shoulder, long skirted coat, hard-cinched waist, tall boots — and none of
the identifiable garments. No tunic, no peaked cap, no lacquered plate, no sword.

**Two retrievals had to be blocked, and both are in `outfits.yaml`:**

- **Imperial officer.** Star Wars officer dress is already WWII-derived. If she
  reads as Imperial, a chain of command is implied that does not exist. She
  commands eight people and a dog.
- **Samurai — the more dangerous one.** Baylan's entire design depends on him not
  reading as a Jedi, and he stands beside her on the right flank in Sc.25. A
  samurai silhouette next to him throws the association onto the wrong character
  at the worst possible moment.

## Physical Design

- **Species: human.** Decided 2026-08-01. The akk dog should be the only
  non-human thing beside her — her authority is borrowed from it, so nothing
  about her body may compete with it for attention.
- **Age: forties.** From the script.
- **Build: powerfully built** — broad through the shoulders and back, heavy
  through the arms, thick-set rather than lean or athletic. Twenty years of the
  work and she can still do it.
- **Height: around 175 cm.** An art department proposal, not a script fact.
  Baylan stands a clear head above her at 198.
- **Handedness: RIGHT.** Decided 2026-08-01. Blaster on her right hip; leash,
  pouches and tools on her left, off the drawing side.
- **Not yet cast.** No reference photograph exists, and the prompts say so
  rather than inventing a likeness.

## Costume and Armour

Full specification in [`outfits.yaml`](outfits.yaml). In summary:

| Element | Note |
|---|---|
| **Long coat** | Good dark hide, skirted panel below the knee, split at the back. Hard square shoulder line |
| **Collar** | Stiff, stands, closed to the throat. Never open, never a lapel |
| **Waist** | Cinched hard by a wide heavy belt — the cinch is what makes the silhouette read |
| **Boots** | Tall, the best in the crew, resoled and cared for |
| **Brow band** | Low, ornate, asymmetric, a small worn crest to one side. **Not a helmet, not a crown, covers none of her face.** The only decorative object on her |
| **Condition** | Good materials, badly weathered. Faded across the shoulders, mended at cuff and hem, mud to the knee, collar worn shiny |

**No insignia, no rank badge, no faction mark, no medal, no braid.** The moment
she carries a real marking, the design collapses — her authority stops being
borrowed.

## Equipment and Hero Props

- **The whistle.** A small worn whistle on a cord at her throat, cheap and much
  handled. **It is her entire command structure**: she whistles and the beast
  comes, she whistles and it kills. It is worth nothing to anybody else, and it
  is the cheapest hero prop in the film.
- **The leash.** Heavy chain or thick strap, **coiled and hung at her belt on her
  left**, never in her hand and never on the animal. Carrying restraint she does
  not need is a display for the crew — the difference between commanding an
  animal and controlling a weapon. Decided 2026-08-01. Something to physically
  drop in the finale.
- **The blaster.** Her right hip, belt-mounted, grip up and angled forward.
  **Clean, serviced and cared for** — unlike everything else in this crew. The one
  thing she spends money keeping right. In Sc.25 it is *"still glowing"* after
  Krellis.

## Performance and Body Language

- She kills a man mid-sentence and **smiles**. Everything else must be built so
  that reads as characteristic rather than shocking.
- Sc.9 opens on her **worried** about her animal, not eating. The affection is
  genuine and is not a performance for subordinates.
- Sc.19's kneeling squad is **compliance through fear**, not discipline.
- She charges Baylan in the finale *"filled with confusion and rage"* — after the
  animal refuses her. She dies confused, which is the correct note.

## Open Questions

- ~~What tack does the akk dog wear, and does she hold or release it?~~
  **Whistle plus a heavy leash she rarely uses**, coiled at her belt and never in
  her hand. Decided 2026-08-01.
- What is her share, and how visibly does she wear it?
- She raised it, implied but unstated. Does anything on the animal predate her —
  older tack, a mark, a scar she did not cause?
- ~~Is she afraid of it?~~ **No** — she raised it. That removes a layer of tension
  and adds a worse one. Decided 2026-08-01.
- **Casting.** No actor reference exists. Until one does, every generated image
  gives her a different face.
- **What does the crest on the brow band depict?** It should be salvage or
  purchase, never an award. Nobody has decided what it is.
- **Does she wear the coat in every scene**, or does it come off for the pursuit
  in Sc.23–24? One costume is currently specified.
