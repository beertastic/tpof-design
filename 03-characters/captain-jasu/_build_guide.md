# Captain Jasu — Outfit Build Guide

**She is 155 cm (5 ft 1 in).** Twenty-eight, slight and fine-boned, and the
smallest adult in the film.

> **THIS FILE IS DERIVED FROM `outfits.yaml`.** That file's `components:` list is
> the source of truth — route, quantity, status and the reasoning for every
> piece. When it changes, this changes. Do not edit this file to record a design
> decision; edit `outfits.yaml` and rewrite this from it.

## Read this first — the superseded guide

**A guide called `Jasu_outfit_build_guide.md.docx` was uploaded to Drive on
2026-08-01 and is wrong.** It was written from photographs rather than from the
design, two days before the v2 rebuild, and it misread five things. If you have a
copy, replace it with this one.

| The old guide said | It is actually |
|---|---|
| A **shorter** wearer, height understated | **155 cm (5 ft 1 in)** — re-cast 2026-08-03 |
| **Ankle-height boots**, "Chelsea or combat" | **TALL** — shaft well up the calf, ending below the knee |
| A whistle as a **belt fob**, "vintage keychain fob" | **Exactly ONE whistle, at her THROAT.** The belt carries none |
| A bought **Etsy leather pauldron** for the mantle | **Stiffened cloth, made not bought.** See the warning below |
| **Hair cuffs / "viking hair rings"** | **Two bone horns**, printed, deliberately mismatched |

Only the trousers advice survived intact — equestrian jodhpurs really are the
right match, and that reasoning is kept below.

## Sizing

**Nothing off the peg fits her, and "looks thrifted" is the one thing this
costume cannot look.** Buy for construction and alter to fit. On Vinted add
*Petite* to filters where available, look at UK 4–6 / XS, and read the seller's
stated inseam and sleeve length rather than trusting the size label.

The base top in particular **must be altered to fit her exactly** once found.

## The build

### 1. Ribbed base top — mock neck, stand collar, front closure
**Bought ×1.** Heavy ribbed knit, dark warm brown.

Search by **construction, not by look** — `ribbed mock neck`, `ribbed base
layer`, `ribbed zip turtleneck`. Ski and thermal base layers and dance wear all
turn up constantly secondhand with the right ribbed texture.

**Must be altered to fit exactly.** £3–8.

### 2. Shoulder mantle
**MADE ×1 — do not buy one.**

**Stiffened cloth** — ribbed, quilted or pleated, holding its shape by
construction. Sharp upswept point at each shoulder, warm dark brown, rising at
the back into the stand collar.

> **NOT leather. NOT printed. NOT a bought pauldron.** The superseded guide
> recommended a ready-made Etsy leather pauldron. `outfits.yaml` records that as
> **"the single most wrong thing it is possible to buy for this costume"** — it
> reads as fantasy armour and collapses her whole silhouette.

### 3. Trousers — quilted outer-thigh panels, knee pads
**Bought ×1.** Dark warm brown.

**Equestrian jodhpurs or breeches are the real-world match.** Contrast knee
patches stitched onto a fabric leg is exactly this construction, and riders
resell them constantly. Search `jodhpurs brown`, `breeches brown`.

Panels and distressing are added in-house afterwards. £5–15.

### 4. Forearm bracers
**Made ×2.** Leather, riveted, buckled, dark.

**Forearm only** — wrist to below the elbow, not continuing up the upper arm.
One of the few places leather is correct on this costume.

### 5. Wide belt, buckle and fittings
**Bought ×1.** Broad leather, plain heavy buckle. Men's belts are often the right
proportion even on a petite frame. Buckle and fittings printed if nothing
suitable turns up. £3–10.

### 6. Belt pouches
**Bought ×2.**

### 7. Holster — belt-mounted, her RIGHT hip
**Made ×1.** Leather, hanging from the belt down the right thigh, grip up and
angled forward for a **right-hand draw**. Built around the A180, not around a
generic pistol.

### 8. A180 blaster pistol
**Printed ×1.** The weapon Jyn Erso carries in Rogue One — reference at
`reference/props/a180.jpg`.

Two-tone: bright polished steel forward, black at the back. Six vent holes near
the muzzle, fluting part way back, mounting rail with a knurled knob, chequered
raked grip.

**IT MUST FINISH BRIGHT AND CLEAN.** Everything else in this production is dulled
and distressed; this is the one thing she looks after.

### 9. Bone horns for the headdress
**Printed ×2.** Pale bone, matte, **yellowed with age**. Never polished, never
carved, never set in metal — they are trophies from what the akk kills, and a
worked finish turns them into jewellery and collapses the reading.

> **THEY MUST NOT MATCH EACH OTHER.** Long and sweeping on one side, shorter
> points on the other. A printer produces a perfect matched pair by default, and
> a matched pair is a manufactured ornament rather than two things taken off two
> kills. This is the printing trap recorded in `Costume-Build-Method.md`.

They sit set into swept-back dark hair, above and behind the ear. **Her hair is
worn down and loose** — never styled, never gathered up.

### 10. Whistle on a cord
**Printed ×1. THE HERO PROP.**

Small, cheap-looking, much handled, **worn at the throat**. It is her entire
command structure — she whistles and the beast comes, she whistles and it kills —
and it is worth nothing to anybody else. It is the most important object she
owns.

> **THERE IS EXACTLY ONE, AND IT IS AT HER THROAT.** The belt carries no second
> whistle. A generated test on 2026-08-03 came back wearing two, and two makes it
> an ornament instead of an instrument.

### 11. Leash — heavy, coiled at her LEFT hip
**Bought ×1.** Heavy strap or chain, coiled and hung at the belt on her left, off
the drawing side.

**Never in her hand and never on the animal.** She is carrying restraint she does
not need, as a display for the crew.

### 12. Boots — tall, close to the calf
**Bought ×1.** Brown leather, practical, genuinely worn.

**TALL — the shaft rises well up the calf and ends below the knee.** Confirmed
against a generated A/B on 2026-08-03. Not ankle boots; that was the v1 error.

Charity shops are the best source and **the wear is wanted** — do not pay extra
for "like new". The best boots in the crew, and looked after.

## What she is not

Nothing about her is insignia, and nothing about her was issued. Everything is
good material and everything is battered: faded across the shoulders, mended at
the cuffs, mud to the knee.

**Not an Imperial officer** — no rank plaque, no code cylinders, no peaked cap,
no gleaming jackboots. She commands eight people and a dog; there is no empire
behind her.

**Not a samurai and not a Jedi** — no lacquered plate, no laced armour panels, no
wide flaring shoulder guards, no horned helmet, no face mask, no robes, no sword.
