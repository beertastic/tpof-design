| **Search** | `tall brown leather boots flat`  ·  `leather riding boots brown`  ·  `knee high leather boots flat heel` |
| **Where** | CHARITY SHOPS FIRST — they are the best source and the cheapest, and the wear is wanted. Vinted and eBay after. |
| **Filter** | Her size, and check the CALF measurement as well as the foot — a tall shaft that will not close is the commonest failure on a fine build. |
| **Spec** | Brown leather, practical, genuinely worn. TALL — the shaft rises well up the calf and ends below the knee. The best boots in the crew, and looked after. |
| **Avoid** | HEEL FLAT, or as near flat as a sourced boot allows — a walking sole, no more than ~25 mm. If a boot is right in every other way, RE-HEEL IT — that is a cobbler's job and it costs less than the boot. Do not pay extra for "like new". |
