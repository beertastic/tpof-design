# The horn authority

`horns-authority-2026-08-04.png` is the scoped reference for **the bone horns and
nothing else.**

It is a crop of `source/artwork/portrait.png`, taken **above the collar so that no
costume is left in frame to compete with the approved front.** That is the method
Shada's make-up plate established: two references may both be attached only when
their scopes do not overlap, and the way you guarantee that is to crop the other
garment out rather than describe it away.

**Attach it whenever the horns are large in frame** — slots 6, 7 and 8, and any
re-roll of the front or the matched views.

## Why the portrait, and not the front or the headdress plate

The spec was settled by the Production Designer on 2026-08-04: **two horns, one
each side, both curved, alike in curve, colour and size, officially different
objects, each a third to a half the height of her head.**

Measured as a fraction of head height:

| Plate | Horns | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **`portrait.png`** | 46% and 53%, one blunt-tipped and one pointed | **The only plate compliant on every point.** This crop |
| `turn-field-front.png` | 38% and 53% | **A true mirrored pair** — flipping one produces the other, verified by test. The size is fine; the objects are not |
| `headdress.png` | three on the visible side | Obeys the *superseded* rule, which asked for a much stronger asymmetry |
| `expression_strip.png` | 20% and 47%, and ridged | Sizes not alike, and banded against a rule that says smooth |

**The approved front must not be used for the horns.** It is authoritative for
the costume and nothing above the collar.
