# Palpatine — Reference Note

> **Not a design asset.** This is a reference note, not a character package. No
> `Character.md`, no `Character-Lock.md`, no prompt pack, no board.

## Appearance in this film

**Scene 12 only**, inside Shin's vision. The breakdown lists him as off-screen
with no dialogue attribution, but his voice delivers the line
*"Execute Order Sixty-Six."*

**Glimpsed, not seen.** He may be partially visible within the vision.

## Design rule

The visions in this film are **associative and deliberately illegible** — see
[`03-characters/baylan/Character-Lock.md`](../baylan/Character-Lock.md). That
applies here with particular force.

- **Do not stage a recognisable Palpatine.** No clear face, no full figure, no
  establishing shot, no throne, no hood pulled back.
- Suggestion only: a shape, a partial silhouette, a colour, at most a fragment.
- **The voice does the work.** It is the most recognisable sound in the franchise
  and needs no visual support.
- If a generated image renders him clearly, it is wrong.

## Rejections

- No hero framing, no full-body reveal, no legible face.
- Nothing that turns the vision into a flashback with a villain in it.
- No costume design work. If he is glimpsed, it is as an impression.
