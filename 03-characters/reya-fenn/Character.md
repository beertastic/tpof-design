# Reya Fenn

> **Production rule:** Refer to the Production Design Bible before any visual development or image generation.

> **Naming:** Formerly listed as `Freya` in an earlier cast list. Same character,
> renamed. Confirmed 2026-07-30.

## Art Department Brief

Mercenary appearing in the Sabacc scene and final clearing sequence.

## Story Function

**A working mercenary with the best entrance in the film**, and one of the four
named crew who die in Baylan's massacre.

| Scene | Beat |
|---|---|
| 10 | **She comes in off patrol in full armour, rifle in both hands, still wet** — and takes the helmet off. *"Her hair tumbles over her shoulders."* Sits down, gets dealt in, and demolishes Nyx's version of the Naboo job with one line: *"You definitely fell on top of him, Nyx!"* |
| 23 | Assigned to the **pincer group** — from the left, with Shada, Yaslo Bis and Merc 4. See [`Crew-Roster.md`](../../04-factions/mercenaries/Crew-Roster.md) |
| 25 | **She speaks first in the finale** — *"Well, well, well. Looks like we got to you first."* Then Shin's blast puts her down, and **she is killed in Baylan's massacre** |

### Corrected 2026-08-01

The design documents had her **killed off screen by Vala's grenade**. She is not:
she is alive, armed and talking in the finale, and dies with the other three when
Baylan turns on the crew. Recorded from script v10.

### What the helmet does

She is the only mercenary the audience meets **as equipment first and a person
second.** Full armour, faceless, carrying a rifle — and then she takes the helmet
off and is somebody who has been walking in the rain for hours and is fed up with
an argument that was already old when she left.

**That reversal is her whole design brief.** The armour has to read as genuinely
anonymous — she should be indistinguishable from any other trooper silhouette in
the doorway — and the face underneath has to be the opposite.

It also means she is the one crew member whose costume must work fully sealed.
Everybody else's kit is assembled and mismatched; hers has to close.
