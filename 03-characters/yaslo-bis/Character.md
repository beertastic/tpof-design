## Art Department Brief

A working mercenary with a speaking part in two scenes. Needs his own costume,
not a kit build — he is in frame and talking, twice, in good light.

## Story Function

| Scene | Beat |
|---|---|
| 10 | **He deals the Sabacc hand.** *"Am I dealing you in?"* to Reya Fenn. Then he is the one who calls Nyx out — *"I saw you on the roof before it happened. You were looking in the complete wrong direction!"* — and gets the biggest laugh in the film |
| 25 | Part of the pincer group holding the escapees. *"Come on — don't make it worse for yourselves."* and *"Looks like we get to do this the fun way."* |
| 25 | **Killed in Baylan's massacre** |

He is in the **pincer group** — Shada, Reya Fenn, Yaslo Bis and Merc 4, sent in
from the left in Scene 23. See
[`Crew-Roster.md`](../../04-factions/mercenaries/Crew-Roster.md).

## What the script gives us

Not much, and what there is, is tonal rather than physical.

**He is comfortable in the crew and enjoys them.** He deals, he takes the mickey
out of Nyx to his face, and the group laughs with him rather than at him. He is
socially central in the one scene where the mercenaries are people rather than a
threat.

**And then he is the one who says *"looks like we get to do this the fun way"***
with blasters pointed at a fifteen-year-old. That is the whole faction thesis in
one character — professionals doing a job, enjoying it, not evil, which is worse.
The Sabacc scene is what makes that line land.

**Design consequence:** do not make him look like a thug. The Sabacc scene has to
be genuinely warm or the finale gains nothing.
