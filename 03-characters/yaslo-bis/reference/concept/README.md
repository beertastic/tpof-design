# `concept/` — Yaslo Bis

## `yaslo-concept-2026-08-05.jpg`

**The Production Designer's own concept**, supplied the day the costume moved to
the 3D team. Part line drawing, part composited head — an idea, not a finished
design, and not a costume authority in the way a fitting photograph is.

**It supersedes the written rules where the two disagree, because it came from
the person the rules were written to serve.** `../../outfits.yaml` has been
corrected on the two points where it contradicted this image; that file is now
reference for the 3D team rather than a build specification.

## What it shows

- **BALD.** Not stated anywhere before.
- **A FULL, LONG BEARD** — heavy, reaching the chest. Also not stated before, and
  it does a lot of the warmth the character needs.
- **A METAL PLATE OVER THE LEFT SIDE OF THE FACE**, covering the brow, the eye
  socket and the cheekbone, wrapping to the temple. **Cables or tubes loop from
  it back around the ear.**
- **THE LEFT EYE IS A RED LENS, AND IT IS LIT.**
- **HEAVY ARMOUR**: layered shoulder pauldrons, a segmented chest and torso
  plate, articulated arm plating to gauntleted fists, a forearm-mounted device
  on his right arm. Small triangular marks on both pauldrons.
- His right side of the face is untouched human skin.

## Two things it overturns

**1. THE LIT RED EYE.** The drafted rule said *"NOTHING LIT, NOTHING glowing,
NOTHING that whirs or reads as new technology."* That was written to keep him
from reading as a machine and to protect the Sabacc scene's warmth. **The
concept lights it deliberately, and the rule has been changed rather than the
concept.**

The characterisation still has to survive it. What protects it here is that the
lit eye is on **one side only**, and the other side — the eye that actually
plays the scene — is human and unobstructed. The warmth comes from the beard,
the open right side and the performance. **If a generation makes the red eye the
subject of the frame, that is the failure to watch for**, not the fact of it
being lit.

*This also touches Baylan.* His gauntlet screen is specified dark and dead
against the later costume's amber readout, and crew-wide gauntlet lights are an
open question in `11-production-tracking/Open-Questions.md`. Yaslo having a lit
red eye does not settle that — a facial prosthetic and a wrist display are
different objects — but the two should be decided knowing about each other.

**2. HE IS HEAVILY ARMOURED.** The drafted rule said *"SOFT, not armoured — the
least plated man in the crew… layered coats and wraps rather than plate."* That
was invented from the faction grammar with no reference to work from, and the
concept is the opposite: pauldrons, chest plate, articulated arms.

**It is a better fit for a 3D print build**, which is presumably why it went that
way — hard plate is what printing is for, and cloth is not.

**What must not be lost with the softness:** the faction rule that *"if two
members look like they were dressed by the same person, it is wrong."* Reya Fenn
is also fully armoured and also 3D printed. **Those two builds must not converge
into a matched pair** — hers is sealed, anonymous and closed against weather;
his is open-faced, personal and marked. That distinction now has to be held by
the 3D team rather than by these documents.

## What is still true from the written spec

- **The facework must not cost him expression.** One side only, the other side
  untouched — the concept honours this and the rule stands.
- **Not a thug.** He is the warm one; the Sabacc scene has to be genuinely warm
  or the finale gains nothing.
- **Nothing issued, nothing matching another crew member.**
- **He is not a `mercenary-kit` build.** Merc 1–4 are allocated elsewhere.

## Open, and now a conversation with the 3D team

- **Which side?** The concept shows the plate on his **left**. Fix it, because it
  drives his blocking, his weapon side and every image of him.
- **The arms.** `Character.md` left the cybernetic extent deliberately unfixed —
  *"face is the decision, arms is a maybe"*. The concept shows armour on the
  arms, not visibly cybernetic arms. Those are different things and the
  distinction is worth making before printing.
- **How bright, and does it ever change?** A lit element on a face is a
  continuity commitment for every shot he appears in.
