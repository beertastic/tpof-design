## Why the YT-2000

Chosen specifically so the ship is **not read as the Millennium Falcon**. The
YT-2000 shares the Corellian YT-series lineage and its industrial, freighter
character, but differs where it matters visually:

- **Centred cockpit** rather than the YT-1300's offset one.
- **Broadly symmetrical hull**, so the silhouette does not carry the Falcon's
  distinctive asymmetry.

The audience should read *"Corellian freighter"* and not *"that ship."*

Verify exact dimensions and specification against production reference before
locking the exterior — the notes here are directional.

## The crew are over capacity

A YT-2000 is a light freighter with a small nominal crew and limited passenger
accommodation. **Ten mercenaries is more than it is meant to hold**, and that is
a design statement rather than a problem.

- Bunks improvised in cargo space. Someone sleeps in a corridor.
- Personal territory marked out in a space with no rooms to spare.
- Gear stowed wherever it fits, which is why the crew's kit is on their bodies.
- **No privacy anywhere.**

This directly supports the Production Design Bible's requirement that interiors
feel *cramped, functional and lived in rather than luxurious* — and it gives a
diegetic reason the crew is ten and not twenty, which removes any need to justify
the headcount.

### It makes Baylan's years worse

He has hidden what he is **in a space where you cannot be alone.** Everyone walks
past everything eventually. That is why the one time we see him practising —
Scene 14, in a makeshift storage area away from the ship — he is found within
minutes.

## Where the akk dog lives

**Proposed: the gun turret.** It sleeps curled inside one of the ship's turret
blisters. Cargo hold is the fallback.

A YT turret is a cramped spherical space — genuinely plausible for a large animal
to curl into, and far more interesting than a pen.

**Why it is the right answer:**

- **They gave up a gun position to a weapon that sleeps.** The animal *is* the
  gun, and nobody on that crew ever had to say so.
- It is absurd and slightly sad, which suits a beast that adores a woman who will
  spend it.
- One turret is therefore **unusable**, permanently. That is a real operational
  cost the crew has simply accepted, and it says as much about Jasu's standing as
  any dialogue could.
- It solves the spatial problem on an over-capacity ship without displacing crew
  bunks — though the hair, the smell and the noise are everyone's problem.

**Not referenced in the script.** A visual beat rather than a plot point, and a
good one — a single shot of an akk dog asleep in a gun blister tells an audience
everything about this crew.

If the turret does not work practically, the **cargo hold** is the fallback, in
which case it has displaced somebody's sleeping space and that person has
feelings about it.

## Design language

Follows the mercenary faction: **nothing issued, everything chosen, maintained
rather than pristine.** The ship is the crew's one shared possession and the only
thing they all own together.

- Visible maintenance access, replacement parts, mismatched paint, service
  markings, fuel staining — per Bible §7.
- Repairs visible rather than hidden. Accumulated over years by people who fix
  things themselves.
- The interior sets the **quality standard for the faction**. This crew can afford
  good salvage, and the ship should show that: worn but sound, not derelict.
- Contrast deliberately with the escapees' ship, which is *rickety* and comes down
  in Scene 2.

## It is stolen in the finale

**Baylan and Shin take the ship.** It lifts away as Vala watches from the ground
in the post-credit scene, and she has no idea who is aboard.

Consequences:

- **The ship survives into Film 2 as theirs.** It is no longer a mercenary vessel;
  it is the thing two people live in.
- **Archive it.** Any physical build, the digital interior, and all reference.
- Baylan has lived aboard it for years and now owns it, having killed everyone
  else who did. Whatever he does or does not change about it in Film 2 is
  characterisation.
- The akk dog's turret is empty. The animal is left behind on the planet.
