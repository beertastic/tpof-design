# Forest World

> Refer to the Production Design Bible before all visual development.

**Never named on screen. Deliberate — do not invent a name.** These people are
nowhere, which is the point. Confirmed 2026-07-30.

## Story Function

The only planet in the film. A chase on foot across roughly one day and night.

## The geography, and why the film happens on foot

**Settled 2026-08-01 by the Production Designer.** The whole shape of the film
comes out of one line of Jasu's in Scene 9, which had no design entry until now:

> The reports say that their ship crashed two clicks from here. **The dampening
> fields used to protect the mines, stop ships flying over.**

That is the engine of the plot and should be treated as load-bearing:

| Element | What it does |
|---|---|
| **The mines** | Never seen, never explained. Somebody else's operation, indifferent to everyone in the film |
| **The dampening fields** | Protect the mines by stopping ships passing overhead |
| **The escapees' ship** | Flew too close, stalled, came down — Sc.2, *"smoke billows from its engines as it drops down past the treeline"* |
| **The crash site** | **Two kilometres** from the mercenary landing. Smoke still rising a day later, in Sc.24 and 25 |
| **The mercenary landing** | They could not overfly the field or set down at the wreck, so they went out and **landed at the first clearing they could find** |

**This is why nobody flies anywhere.** The dampening field is the reason a crew
with a working freighter hunts four people through trees for a day instead of
tracking them from the air in twenty minutes. Without it the film has a hole in
it; with it, everything from Scene 3 onward follows.

It also means **the crash was an accident of navigation, not pursuit.** Nobody
shot them down. They flew into a field protecting a mine that has nothing to do
with them — the same shape as the accident that triggered their escape in the
first place. The galaxy is not paying attention to these people.

## The three zones

```
        [ THE MINES ]  ·  dampening field  ·  never seen
                 \
                  \   the escapees fly too close and stall
                   \
              [ CRASH SITE ]  ·  smoke rising all film
                     |
                     |  ~2 km
                     |
     [ LANDING CLEARING ]——————[ DENSE FOREST ]——————>
      mercenaries land here      escapees run in here
      outskirts. open enough     Sc.8 onward
      for a YT-2000
```

| Zone | Character | Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| **The outskirts** | Thinner forest, clearings big enough to land in. The mercenaries | 3, 5, 7, 9, 13–15, 19, 20 |
| **The dense forest** | Close, wet, roots underfoot, no sightlines. The escapees | 8, 16–18, 21, 22 |
| **The clearing** | Where it ends | 24, 25 |

**The escapees went deeper; the mercenaries came from outside.** That is a real
visual distinction and it should be built. The escapee material is close, tangled
and claustrophobic. The mercenary material has sky in it.

## Set Dressing

Wet temperate forest. Mulch-covered ground — Shin *"tosses and turns on the
mulch-covered ground"*. Roots that catch feet, Sc.8. Trees *"eerily still"* in the
open space at Sc.8.

The sky is a real element and it belongs to Shin. Sc.8 gives her *"a watercolour
portrait of oranges and purples"* and stars appearing, and she has never seen it
before.

## Assets this implies that do not exist yet

- **The crash site.** No location document. It is briefed in Sc.9, its smoke is
  set dressing in Sc.24 and 25, and it may be where Baylan and Shin are heading
  in the last shot.
- **The mines.** Referenced once and never seen — the same treatment as Prodona.
  Probably needs nothing beyond this entry, but somebody should decide that rather
  than forget it.

## Open

- Does the dampening field have any visible effect at all — instrument failure, a
  horizon glow, nothing? Currently nothing, which is the cheap and probably
  correct answer.
- **Can the mercenary ship lift off at the end?** It landed outside the field, so
  yes. But if Baylan and Shin head *toward* the crash site in the last shot they
  are walking toward the mine and away from the only working ship.
