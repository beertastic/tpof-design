# Prodona

> **Naming:** The forest world itself is never named on screen. **Prodona is**, by
> Krellis, in Scene 8. It is the only place name spoken in the film.

> **Production rule:** Refer to the Production Design Bible before any visual development.

## What it is

**A spaceport city on this planet. Busy, crowded, and somewhere you can slip
away.**

Not a haven. Nobody is waiting to help them. There is no settlement that takes in
escaped slaves and no organised sanctuary. Prodona is simply **large enough and
busy enough to vanish into**, and there may — possibly, thinly — be someone there
who knows someone.

## The important part

**It is the only option besides the woods.**

That is the whole of it. They are not running toward a promised land; they are
running toward the one place on the map that is not forest. Krellis directs the
route because there is no other route to direct.

**Freedom, in this film, is anonymity.** Not rescue, not refuge — a crowd big
enough that nobody looks at you twice. That is what four people die trying to
reach.

## Why this matters to the pursuit

**It puts a clock on the mercenaries.**

If the escapees reach a working spaceport they are gone — into a crowd, onto a
ship, untraceable within the hour. Jasu's crew is not merely hunting them, it is
**racing them**, and the forest is the only place they can still be caught.

That should be visible in scenes 20 to 23: urgency, not leisure. The pursuit is
against a deadline, and the deadline is a city.

## Design language, if it is ever built

Everything about Prodona is the **opposite of the rest of the film**:

| The film | Prodona |
|---|---|
| Wet forest, mud, trees | Hard surfaces, traffic, noise |
| Twelve people | Thousands |
| Everyone knows everyone | Nobody looks at anybody |
| Silence and stalking | Crowds and engines |

Standard Bible rules apply — industrial salvage, visible repair, working
architecture built around necessity, no pristine futurism. A frontier port that
works, not one that impresses.

## Status

**Referenced only. Never seen in this film.** No design work is required unless
it becomes a Film 2 location.

They do not reach it. Krellis, who knew the way, is shot in the clearing.
