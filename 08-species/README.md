## Two different design problems

**Species** — sentient peoples with culture, clothing, tools and craftsmanship.
Shada's serpentine ancestry is species work, and it lives in her character
document rather than here because it is inseparable from her design.

**Creatures** — animals. Designed for weight, anatomy and behaviour rather than
culture, and usually built as physical or 3D assets rather than described.

They currently share this folder because there is one creature. If creatures grow
past two or three, split them into their own top-level category.

## When an asset already exists

The akk dog was modelled and rigged before any documentation existed. Where a
built asset exists, **the asset is canonical** — documents and generated imagery
describe it, never the reverse. Reference plates rendered from the asset go in
that entry's `reference/` folder and are committed, unlike the large regenerable
renders elsewhere in this repository.
