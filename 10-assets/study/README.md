# Study material

**Drop stills, frame grabs and screenshots from other productions here. Nothing
in this folder is committed except this file.**

## Why it is gitignored

**This repository is public.** An unauthenticated fetch of
`raw.githubusercontent.com` returns 200 — verified 2026-08-01. Anything committed
here is published, indexed, and kept in Git history forever even if deleted later.

Studying a copyrighted costume to design an original one is ordinary practice and
entirely normal for a fan film. **Republishing the still is a different act**, and
it is the one that causes trouble. So the picture stays local and the *conclusions*
get committed.

## What to commit instead

The design note the still produced. That is the thing of value, and it survives
the still being lost:

> **Green stitching, 2026-08-01.** The thread holding the chevron panels is a
> dark greyed-off green — the only colour on him, and only findable in close-up.
> Plants the colour he is known for later without anyone being able to name it
> on a first watch.

**Write down the derived decision, never the source image.**

## Working with a still

The useful question is not *"what does it look like"* but **what has to be true
eighteen years earlier**. A costume this film establishes must be:

- **Cheaper.** He has no money, no status and no one to impress.
- **Working.** Every element does a job, or it is not on him.
- **Unremarkable.** Nobody around him may suspect what he was.

So the transfer is rarely a shape. It is usually a **material, a colour, a
construction method or a proportion** — the things a person keeps because
replacing them costs money, not because they mean something.
