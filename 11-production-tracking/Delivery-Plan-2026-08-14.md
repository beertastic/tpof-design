# Delivery Plan — Friday 14 August 2026

**Written 2026-08-05.** Nine days. **3–5 hours a day, every day including
weekends.** The target is *as much as possible*, in an order that means whatever
does not get done is the least important thing.

## The arithmetic

| | |
|---|---|
| Total images planned | **248** |
| Done | **45** — Shada 21, Jasu 21, Baylan 2 (superseded), Vala 1 |
| **Outstanding** | **~203** |
| Days | **9** |
| **Required rate** | **~23 images/day** |

**At five minutes a generation — paste, attach, wait, judge, save — 23 images is
under two hours.** Three to five hours a day therefore buys roughly **two to
three attempts per image**, which is the real headroom in this plan.

**IT IS ACHIEVABLE. It is achievable only if design iteration stops.**

## The one rule this plan depends on

Two data points from 2026-08-05, and they are the whole plan:

| | Generations for one front | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Baylan** | ~16, still not approved | The costume was being **designed** in the loop — twenty rule changes in a day |
| **Vala** | **1**, essentially right | A **real costume existed** to describe |

203 images at Vala's rate is comfortable. At Baylan's rate it is 3,200
generations and no number of hours makes it possible.

> ## THE RULE
>
> **Accept the second generation.** If a plate is wrong twice, it is a DESIGN
> problem, not a prompt problem — park it, log it, move on, and come back only
> if there is time at the end.
>
> **The exception is continuity, not taste.** Handedness, weapon placement,
> palette, a planted element or anything in `Planted-Elements.md` gets fixed
> however long it takes. How much a hem frays does not.

## Division of labour

**Nothing in the plan should ever have you waiting on me.** Everything I can do
without you is front-loaded to the night before.

| Me, unattended | You, in your 3–5 hours |
|---|---|
| Draft every unanswered design question and bring a list to rule on | Rule on the drafts — in batches, not one at a time |
| Write `components:` for the eight characters without one | **Generate. This is the bottleneck and almost all of your time** |
| Regenerate prompts, verify attachments, check the trim reports | Judge each plate: accept, or re-roll once |
| File, rename and verify every image you produce | Approve fronts |
| Build sheets, guides and boards as fronts approve | |

## What gets cut, in this order

If the plan slips, it slips from the bottom:

1. **`tone-collage`** for everyone — it is a share sheet, nobody builds from it
2. **Narrative and environment slots** — context, not specification
3. **Mercenary Kit turnarounds** — background family, and Merc 1 already has no
   turnaround by decision
4. **Baylan's `working-coat` views** — slot 13 is a separate garment and is
   already deprioritised

## What must not be cut

- **One approved front per character.** Without it there is no costume record at
  all, and every downstream asset is untrustworthy
- **`components:` and the build sheets.** They are what the costume department
  actually works from — the images are how it is checked
- **Anything in `Planted-Elements.md`.** The clasp, the chevrons, the blaster,
  the green thread, the coat. Those carry to Films 2 and 3 and cannot be redone

## Standing risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **A character turns into Baylan** — design surfacing during generation | The two-attempt rule. Park and log |
| **Vala's actor permission** — nothing of hers may be published | Sheets and boards can be built; publishing waits |
| **The 3D team's two** — Reya and Yaslo need images, not design | Generate, accept, hand over. Do not iterate |
| **The repo is private now** — every `raw.githubusercontent` fallback URL is dead | Attach from `prompts/attach/` folders. If a reply claims it fetched a URL, the image is untrustworthy |
| **`regen` does `git add -A`** | Commit unrelated work before running it |
